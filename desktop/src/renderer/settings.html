Highlight text in any app, press your hotkey, and ask about it.

Hotkey

Popup

On Wayland, apps can't place their own windows, so Kiki adds a small GNOME Shell extension that opens the popup next to the pointer. It also takes Kiki's screenshots of the window you were in, around the pointer. GNOME loads it the next time you log in.

Context

Before you send a question, the popup shows what will be shared, and you can remove any of it.

Turns on GNOME accessibility support so browsers and Electron apps share their text with Kiki. Restart the browser afterwards.

Accessibility lets Kiki read the selected text. Screen Recording lets it see the page around it.

Connected accounts

Lets Kiki add events to Google Calendar, add tasks, save Gmail drafts and send emails, each only after you approve its card, and check your calendar and tasks when you ask. Kiki never reads your email. Your Google sign-in stays on this computer and never goes to the Kiki server.

Personality

Applies to new conversations. Your prompt is sent to your Kiki server with each one.
