    Popup

    On Wayland, apps can't place their own windows, so Kiki adds a small GNOME Shell extension that opens the popup next to the pointer. It also takes Kiki's screenshots of the window you were in, around the pointer. GNOME loads it the next time you log in.

    Context

    Before you send a question, the popup shows what will be shared, and you can remove any of it.

    Computer control

    Kiki opens pages in a hidden browser of its own (not yours) to read them and work on them. It shows the browser only when you ask to see the page, or when you need to do something there yourself, like log in.

    Whenever Kiki clicks, types, scrolls or pastes in your windows, a banner at the top of the screen says so; press its Stop button or Esc to stop it. Anything that sends, buys, deletes or changes something waits for you on a card, unless you turn that off below.

    Kiki clicks any button or link you ask for (likes, follows, sends, deletes included) and writes into your apps and forms right away: edit, changes, fill and press cards are applied as soon as Kiki proposes them, and show as done, with Undo where there is one. Passwords, card and ID numbers, payments, and calendar, task and email cards still wait for you. A page can't ask Kiki to do things, but a model can misread one: keep this off unless you watch Kiki work, and use Esc or Stop to halt it.

    Turns on GNOME accessibility support so browsers and Electron apps share their text with Kiki. Restart the browser afterwards.

    Accessibility lets Kiki read the selected text. Screen Recording lets it see the page around it.

    Connected accounts

    Lets Kiki add events to Google Calendar, add tasks, save Gmail drafts and send emails, each only after you approve its card, and check your calendar and tasks when you ask. Kiki never reads your email. Your Google sign-in stays on this computer.

    Personality

    Applies to new conversations. Your prompt goes to the model with each question.
